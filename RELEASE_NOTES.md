# Release Notes

## Unreleased

## v0.2.0

### Added

- **The RapidMX ACME certificate authority: a standalone service that issues S/MIME certificates for e-mail addresses.** Built because the public CA the server's enrollment defaulted to (`acme.castle.cloud`) never finished a request (stuck in "verification" for more than 24 hours on the live test server). It speaks ACME (RFC 8555) with the RFC 8823 `email-reply-00` challenge, so `@rapidmx/restapi`'s `Rfc8823AcmeSigningCertificateEnrollment` can use it by changing `mail:pki:rfc8823:directory_url` to `https://acme.rapidmx.io/directory`; it is also tested against the independent `acme-client` package.
  - **Signing, encryption or combined certificates**, chosen by the key usage in the CSR (RFC 8823 section 3.3) and optionally an order `profile`. 90 days by default, `emailProtection` only, `CN=<address>` with an `rfc822Name` SAN; nothing but the public key is taken from the CSR.
  - **Mailbox validation** by a DKIM-signed reply to a DKIM-signed challenge e-mail. The CA verifies the reply's DKIM signature itself (`d=` must be the From domain), the sender and the digest, sends one e-mail per authorization, never reuses an authorization, checks CAA `issuemail` and that the domain can receive mail, and never answers inbound mail.
  - **Let's Encrypt-style rate limits** (token buckets in Redis, in memory without it) with `Retry-After` and `rateLimited` problem documents that link to `/rate-limits`, renewals exempt via `replaces`, plus limits on the verification e-mails one address, domain or account can cause, so the CA cannot be used to mail-bomb someone.
  - **Endpoints for validating what it issued:** issuer certificates (DER for AIA, PEM), the roots to trust, chains and issuer public keys as JWKs (`/ca`, `/ca/*`), a CRL per issuer, an OCSP responder, certificate look-up by serial, and ARI renewal information.
  - **Key custody:** issuing CA keys in a file (optionally passphrase-encrypted) or in an OpenBao/Vault Transit key; `yarn ca:init` creates the hierarchy (root belongs offline). Not verified against a real OpenBao.
  - **Mail:** outbound through any SMTP relay with DKIM; replies arrive on the CA's own SMTP listener or through an HTTP ingest route behind a mail bridge.
  - **Trust model:** the root is not in any OS, browser or mail-client trust store and the CA is not audited; certificates verify only where the RapidMX root (`/ca/roots.pem`) is trusted. See `docs/CPS.md`.
  - **Internationalized addresses (SMTPUTF8, RFC 6531).** An address with a non-ASCII local part gets a certificate with an `SmtpUTF8Mailbox` subject alternative name (RFC 8398) and CSRs may carry one; an IDN domain is handled as A-labels everywhere (order, authorization, rate limits, DNS look-ups, SMTP envelope) and the challenge e-mail and DKIM alignment work for both. One canonical form (NFC local part, lower-case A-label domain) decides whether an order, a CSR and a rate-limit bucket mean the same mailbox.
  - **Operator revocation and account suspension API** (`/admin`, bearer `acme.admin_secret`, absent without one): search certificates by address, serial, account or key, revoke one or a selection (with `dryRun`), suspend an account (cancelling its pending orders, optionally revoking its certificates) and reinstate it. Every action is logged with the operator's name; failed attempts are rate limited; the Helm chart generates the secret and never routes `/admin` publicly.
  - **DNSSEC validation of CAA.** The CA validates CAA answers itself (`src/lib/dnssec`: signature chain from the IANA root trust anchors, NSEC/NSEC3 denial of existence, DS-based detection of unsigned zones) at new-order and again at finalize, and refuses the order when validation fails or cannot be completed instead of falling back to an unvalidated answer. New setting `acme.dns.dnssec` (`validate` by default; `off` is refused outside development); the resolvers in `acme.dns.servers` must pass DNSSEC records through and be reachable on UDP and TCP port 53.
  - **Certificate expiry reminders.** The contacts of the ACME account that holds a certificate are e-mailed 1 week, 3 days and 1 day before it expires, on the day it expires, and 1 day and 1 week after if it has not been renewed. Nothing is sent once a newer valid certificate for the same address and type exists, the certificate is revoked, or the account is deactivated or has no usable contact. A milestone is claimed with a conditional update so replicas never send it twice, a failed relay is retried for up to a day, a missed reminder is never sent a day late, and one e-mail per contact lists everything of the account that is due. Each contact address has a daily budget (`reminderMailsPerContactDay`, 48/day) because anyone can name any address as a contact. Sent from `acme.mail.from` through the existing relay and DKIM key; `acme.reminders.enabled` (default true) and `acme.reminders.batch_size` configure it. Certificates issued before this are scheduled on first run.
  - Not implemented yet: external account binding, Ed25519 subject keys. Not verified against a real Redis, a real inbound MTA or a real mail client trust import.
